      Rules
      Bracket sets the limits (999 = unlimited). Oct 21 2025 rules, unchanged Feb 9 2026: B1–2 no Game Changers, B3 up to 3; no mass land denial below B4; B1 no extra turns, B2–3 few (1 and 2 here are interpretations); no 2-card infinites in B1–2, none early in B3 (0 here is stricter); tutors unrestricted. Game Changers use Scryfall's game_changer field. Commander legality always wins.
      Advanced
      Card score = syn·min(tag synergy, 3) + pop·(1 − log₁₀ EDHREC rank / log₁₀ 40000) + (quality + power·pref(bracket))·cardPower + curve·fit(MV) + owned − price·min(3, price ÷ reference) − bracket·(limited flags) + seeded noise. pref = −0.3, 0.2, 0.6, 1.3, 2 for B1–B5; fit(MV) = 1 − |MV − target| / (4 below, 3 above); price counts only with a budget. Deck score = Σ card scores + pair synergy·Σ 2√(enablers × payoffs) per theme − 2·role·(role slots short) − 8·curve·(drift of average MV beyond ±0.15). The optimiser swaps until no single swap, or budget-neutral pair of swaps, raises the deck score.
      Basics split by color-pip share. Auto nonbasic counts by colors (0–5): 8, 7, 14, 17, 19, 21. Variance adds a seeded ±value to each score; same seed and settings give the same deck.
